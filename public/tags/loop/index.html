Manistee River Trail Loop

Distance24 mi Nights2 DateJun 2026 Total cost$38 Manistee River Trail Loop is one of the best beginner-friendly overnights in the Lower Peninsula, and it barely dents your wallet. Here’s how the trip went, and exactly what it cost. The route The loop follows the Manistee River through old-growth pine, crossing two suspension bridges and passing three backcountry campsites spaced roughly 8 miles apart. Elevation change is moderate and the trail underfoot is soft, so it’s a forgiving first overnight if you’re newer to multi-day hiking. Water access from the river is reliable the whole way, which kept my pack weight down since I never carried more than a liter at a time. ...
