Isle Royale on a budget

Isle Royale is Michigan’s most expensive backpacking destination by far — almost entirely because of ferry costs, not the park itself. Here’s where the money actually goes, and where you can cut it. The real cost driver: getting there Isle Royale is only reachable by ferry or seaplane, and this is where nearly your entire budget goes. The NPS-run Ranger III ferry from Houghton has historically run in the $70-80 range per adult, one-way — meaning round-trip ferry transportation alone can easily be your single biggest trip expense, well above gear, food, or park fees combined. Michigan permits and passes, explained

Michigan’s permit system is simpler than most states, but the terminology trips people up. Here’s what you actually need, and when. Recreation Passport This is Michigan’s state park vehicle entry pass — required for any vehicle entering a state park, state recreation area, DNR boat launch, or state forest campground. Resident cost: $15/year when purchased at license plate registration renewal ($29 for a two-year plate, $7 for motorcycles) Nonresident cost: around $41/year, or roughly $11/day Buying it later (at a park gate or DNR office instead of at renewal) adds a $5 convenience fee It’s tied to your vehicle, not your person — two cars means two passports, or carpool. ...
