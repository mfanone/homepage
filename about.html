About

Mark Martin-Fanone

I'm a Michigan-based backpacker focused on getting more people onto the state's trails without spending a lot to do it. This site covers trip reports, budget gear advice, and guides built around Michigan's specific rules, permits, and land access — the stuff that makes backpacking here different from the PCT or AT playbook you'll find everywhere else.

Have a question, a correction, or a trail I should hike? Reach out at you@martin-fanone.org.
